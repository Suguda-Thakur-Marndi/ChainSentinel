# RiskWise 2.0 — Phase 7 Step 3: Risk Evidence & Assessment Integration

## 1. Objective

Phase 7 Step 3 implements the formal evidence-traceability and assessment integration layer for the RiskWise 2.0 Risk Engine:

$$\text{NormalizedRiskSignal} \longrightarrow \text{RiskEvidence} \longrightarrow \text{RiskFactor} \longrightarrow \text{RiskScore} \longrightarrow \text{RiskAssessment} \longrightarrow \text{RiskExplanation}$$

The core purpose of this layer is to ensure every deterministic risk evaluation produced by the platform is completely auditable and traceable back to the exact normalized signals, external sources, and quality constraints that caused it.

A Risk Assessment generated by Step 3 answers:
1. **What risk was detected?** Categorical risk level and bounded composite score ($[0.0, 100.0]$).
2. **Which risk factors produced it?** Domain-evaluated contributors with deterministic identities.
3. **Which normalized signals support each factor?** Lineage linked directly to Phase 6 `NormalizedRiskSignal` identifiers and fingerprints.
4. **What source produced each signal?** Authoritative primary provider and external feed attribution.
5. **Was the source observation REAL, ESTIMATED, or SIMULATED?** Explicit observation classification.
6. **What confidence and quality was assigned?** Numeric certainty $[0.0, 1.0]$ and canonical validation state (`VALID`, `PARTIAL`, `INVALID`).
7. **Were there conflicts?** Preserved multi-source disagreements with applied uncertainty penalty multipliers.
8. **How did each factor contribute to the final score?** Point-by-point breakdown via `FactorContribution` recording raw contribution $c_k$ and compounding delta $\Delta S_k$.
9. **What is the final risk level?** Standard platform severity taxonomy (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`).
10. **What limitations affected the assessment?** Structured caveats detailing synthetic modeling, estimation, missing location, or stale data.
11. **Can the entire result be deterministically reproduced?** Stable SHA-256 semantic fingerprinting independent of volatile runtime parameters.

## 3. Evidence Contract

The `RiskEvidence` contract (`api/app/risk_engine/evidence.py`) represents an immutable, traceable evidence unit linking an evaluated `RiskFactor` back to its `NormalizedRiskSignal` lineage:

- `evidence_id`: UUIDv5 generated deterministically from `organization_id`, `signal_id`, and `provider`.
- `normalized_signal_id`: Foreign lineage key referencing `NormalizedRiskSignal.signal_id`.
- `organization_id`: Tenant boundary isolation scope.
- `factor_id`: Deterministic identifier of the parent `RiskFactor` if linked.
- `source`: Primary external source taxonomy identifier (e.g. `weather_noaa`).
- `provider`: Ingestion provider service name (e.g. `noaa`, `tomtom`, `aisstream`).
- `source_type`: Observation nature (`REAL`, `ESTIMATED`, `SIMULATED`).
- `event_time`: Timezone-aware UTC timestamp of the real-world condition.
- `relevance`: Typed `EvidenceRelevance` or legacy float weight.
- `fingerprint`: Semantic fingerprint of the underlying signal.
- `quality`: Canonical quality validation classification (`VALID`, `PARTIAL`, `INVALID`).
- `has_conflict`: Boolean flag indicating unresolved multi-source conflict.
- `conflicts`: List of preserved conflicting observations and details.
- `location`: Structured geographic context (`latitude`, `longitude`, `location_name`, `country_code`, `region`).
- `limitations`: Evidence-level caveats (e.g. simulated origin, missing coordinates).
- `provenance`: Lineage dictionary containing `canonical_event_id`, `raw_event_id`, `provider_event_id`, `correlation_id`, and `trace_id`.
- `supporting_sources`: Corroborating external source records preserved from normalization.

---

## 4. Factor-to-Evidence Relationship

Every `RiskFactor` generated by domain evaluators is linked to one or more `RiskEvidence` records:
- `factor.evidence`: Complete list of strongly typed `RiskEvidence` instances.
- `factor.evidence_ids`: Deterministic list of evidence identifiers auto-synchronized with `factor.evidence`.
- `ev.factor_id`: Back-reference linking evidence back to its parent `factor_id`.
- `ev.organization_id`: Synchronized tenant scope matching `factor.organization_id`.

A risk factor cannot exist without traceable evidence unless explicitly modeled as a documented system-generated factor (such as telemetry pipeline latency alerts).

---

## 5. Score Traceability & FactorContribution

The `RiskScore` contract (`api/app/risk_engine/contract.py`) explains how each factor influenced the final score via `FactorContribution`:
- `factor_id`: Identifier of contributing factor.
- `factor_type`: Category classification.
- `name`: Human-readable title.
- `raw_contribution`: Normalized contribution $c_k \in [0.0, 1.0]$.
- `weighted_contribution`: Exact score points $\Delta S_k \in [0.0, 100.0]$ added during compound aggregation.
- `rank`: Deterministic priority rank ($1 = \text{primary risk driver}$).
- `evidence_ids`: Traceable evidence identifiers supporting this factor.
- `severity`: RiskLevel severity.
- `confidence`: Certainty weight.

The score aggregation strictly consumes the output of the Step 2 `BaselineRiskScoreAggregator` Diminishing Marginal Compound Aggregation formula:
$$S_1 = 100.0 \times c_1$$
$$\Delta S_k = (100.0 - S_{k-1}) \times \left(c_k \times \frac{0.50}{1.0 + 0.20 \times (k - 1)}\right)$$
$$S_{\text{final}} = \min(100.0, S_1 + \sum_{k=2}^N \Delta S_k)$$

## 6. Assessment Contract

`RiskAssessment` (`api/app/risk_engine/contract.py`) serves as the authoritative, comprehensive evaluation snapshot:
- `assessment_id`: Deterministic UUIDv5 based on tenant, scope, signal IDs, and evaluation hour.
- `organization_id`: Tenant scope.
- `evaluated_at`: Timezone-aware UTC instant of evaluation.
- `scope`: Evaluation scope (`GLOBAL`, `SHIPMENT`, `SUPPLIER`, `PORT`, `ROUTE`).
- `scope_entity_id`: Optional target entity ID.
- `scope_entity_type`: Optional target `EntityType`.
- `overall_score`: Calculated `RiskScore` with `factor_contributions`.
- `score`: Convenience property exposing `overall_score.score`.
- `risk_level`: Categorical classification (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`).
- `probability`: Strictly `None` (unfabricated).
- `impact`: Strictly `None` (unfabricated).
- `confidence`: Minimum confidence certainty across factors.
- `primary_factor_id`: Deterministic primary driver identifier.
- `primary_factor`: Primary `RiskFactor` instance.
- `factors`: Deterministically ordered list of all evaluated `RiskFactor` records.
- `evidence`: Deduplicated, sorted list of all underlying `RiskEvidence` traces.
- `source_summary`: Comprehensive `AssessmentSourceSummary`.
- `limitations`: Documented caveats and uncertainty reasons.
- `conflicts`: Preserved provider disagreements with 0.85 uncertainty penalty.
- `source_signals`: Source `signal_id` list evaluated.
- `fingerprint`: Reproducible SHA-256 semantic hash.
- `metadata`: Preserved diagnostic telemetry and tracing identifiers.

## 7. Primary Driver Selection

The primary risk driver is selected deterministically using a strict 5-element ordering rule:
1. **Weighted contribution** (descending): Factor contributing highest direct or compounded risk.
2. **Severity** (descending): `CRITICAL` (4) > `HIGH` (3) > `MEDIUM` (2) > `LOW` (1).
3. **Confidence** (descending): Higher certainty observation breaks ties.
4. **Evidence quality** (descending): `VALID` (2) > `PARTIAL` (1) > `INVALID` (0).
5. **Factor ID** (ascending): Stable lexicographical tie-breaker.

No randomness, no wall-clock time, and no LLMs are involved in primary driver selection.

---

## 8. Deterministic Factor Ordering

All factors in `RiskAssessment.factors` and `RiskScore.factors` follow the identical 5-element ordering rule implemented in `sort_factors_deterministically()`. The order never depends on database insertion order, dictionary traversal, or network response timing.

## 9. Source Summary & Independent Source Counting

`AssessmentSourceSummary` tracks:
- `evidence_count`: Total evaluated evidence traces.
- `independent_sources_count`: Unique deduplicated provider/source origins.
- `real_sources_count`: Count of unique REAL observation sources.
- `estimated_sources_count`: Count of unique ESTIMATED observation sources.
- `simulated_sources_count`: Count of unique SIMULATED observation sources.
- `corroborating_sources_count`: Count of external corroborating sources.
- `conflicts_count`: Count of detected multi-source conflicts.
- `sources`: Sorted unique source names.
- `providers`: Sorted unique provider names.

---

## 10. Duplicate vs Corroboration

The integration layer enforces strict distinction between duplicate feeds and independent corroboration:
- **Duplicate Evidence**: Multiple events from the same provider (e.g. NOAA ping repeated 5 times) increment evidence count but remain **1 independent source**.
- **Corroborating Evidence**: Independent confirmation from distinct external providers (e.g. NOAA + UK Met Office) increments both independent sources and corroborating sources count.
- **Simulated Scenarios**: `SIMULATED` sources are strictly barred from being counted as real-world corroboration.

---

## 11. Conflict Representation & Retention

When multi-source conflicts occur (e.g. contradictory weather or speed observations):
- Signals and evidence are never silently dropped or chosen arbitrarily.
- Conflict metadata is preserved in `assessment.conflicts` including conflicting values, provider names, and timestamps.
- The authoritative Step 2 conflict uncertainty multiplier of **0.85** is applied to factor contributions.
- Evidence relevance is tagged as `EvidenceRelevance.CONFLICTING`.

---

## 12. Quality & Limitations

Assessment limitations are aggregated transparently:
- `SIMULATED`: Notes synthetic scenario origin.
- `ESTIMATED`: Notes modeled or inferred data.
- `PARTIAL`: Notes incomplete attributes and specific reason codes (e.g. `MISSING_COORDINATES`).
- `Missing Location`: Highlights signals without spatial coordinates.
- `Stale Timestamp`: Highlights observations older than 24 hours relative to evaluation time.
- `Conflict`: Documents source disagreement and applied 0.85 discount.

---

## 13. Tenant Isolation Enforcement

Tenant boundaries are enforced strictly across all contracts and operations:
- Every `RiskEvidence`, `RiskFactor`, `RiskScore`, and `RiskAssessment` records `organization_id`.
- `RiskEvaluationContext` rejects cross-tenant signals (`TenantMismatchError`).
- `RiskEvidence.from_normalized_signal` rejects mismatched tenant scopes.
- `BaselineRiskScoreAggregator` validates that all factors and evidence match context `organization_id`.
- Cross-tenant evidence lookup, factor linking, and assessment assembly are strictly impossible.

---

## 14. Deterministic Identities & Semantic Fingerprinting

Reproducibility is guaranteed via deterministic UUIDv5 and SHA-256 hashing:
- `evidence_id`: UUIDv5 based on `organization_id:evidence:{signal_id}:{provider}`.
- `factor_id`: UUIDv5 based on `organization_id:factor:{factor_type}:{sorted_evidence_ids}`.
- `assessment_id`: UUIDv5 based on `organization_id:assessment:{scope}:{sorted_signals}:{eval_hour}`.
- `fingerprint`: SHA-256 based on `org:scope:sorted_signals:sorted_factors:score:risk_level`. Volatile timestamps and request tracing IDs are excluded from the semantic hash.

---

## 15. Observability

Operational tracing fields (`correlation_id`, `trace_id`, `evaluation_duration_ms`) are preserved in assessment and evidence metadata for distributed tracing while remaining separate from deterministic semantic fingerprints. No secrets or credentials are ever exposed.

---

## 16. Explanation Integration

`RiskExplanation` (`api/app/risk_engine/explainability.py`) generates structured, human-readable summaries using deterministic rule-based formatting:
- Score and categorical risk level.
- Primary risk driver with factor contribution.
- Factor explanations with exact rank, weighted contribution, and supporting evidence IDs.
- Summary of independent sources, detected conflicts, and operational limitations.
- Zero generative LLM dependencies.
